What is Mendacium?

Mendacium provides synthetic and labeled datasets for training AI models to detect and analyze psychological operations (PSYOPs) and coercive communication. Built for researchers, defense labs, and AI safety teams.

What we provide
  • Synthetic PSYOP datasets
  • Labeled manipulative language examples
  • Coercive communication patterns
Who we serve
  • Defense and intelligence contractors
  • Academic disinformation researchers
  • AI safety and content moderation teams
  • Civil society and NGOs countering manipulation
